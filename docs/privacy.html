Privacy Policy — Recovery Companion

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Recovery Companion ("the app") is a private 12-step recovery diary. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

The short version

Your data never leaves your device. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network requests.

What the app stores

  • Your sobriety start date
  • Your progress through the 12 steps
  • Journal entries, including the mood you select
  • Support contacts you add (name, phone number, role)

All of this is stored only on your device, encrypted at rest using your operating system's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). We — the developers — cannot see, access, or recover any of it.

What the app does NOT do

  • No data is collected, transmitted, sold, or shared with anyone
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising
  • No account creation or sign-in

Phone calls and text messages

When you tap a call or text button (for a support contact or a helpline), the app opens your phone's dialer or messaging app. The call or message is handled entirely by your phone — the app does not see or record it.

Data deletion

Deleting a journal entry or contact in the app removes it immediately. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored data.

Children

The app is intended for adults in recovery and is not directed at children under 13.

Medical disclaimer

Recovery Companion is a personal diary and support tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and is not a substitute for professional care. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Alcoholics Anonymous World Services or any 12-step fellowship.
