Privacy Policy

Recovery Companion · Last updated 18 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Recovery Companion ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information. Recovery Companion is a personal journaling and 12-step recovery support tool. We understand that the information you record in this App is deeply personal, and we treat it accordingly.

The short version: Your journal entries, step work, and account information are stored securely so they can sync across your devices. When you use the optional AI reflection feature, the text you submit is sent to OpenAI to generate a response. We never sell your data, and we never use your recovery content for advertising.

Who we are

Recovery Companion is operated by the App's developer. For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at aidan.koetaan@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account, we collect and store your email address and authentication credentials so you can sign in and access your data across devices.
  • Recovery and journal content. This includes the journal entries, reflections, sponsor notes, sobriety information, and 12-step progress you choose to enter. Because this content relates to addiction recovery, we treat it as sensitive personal information.
  • AI reflection inputs. If you use the optional AI journaling / reflection feature, the text you submit for reflection is processed to generate a response.
  • Technical data. Basic technical information (such as device type and error/diagnostic logs) may be processed to keep the App working and to fix bugs.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To store your journal and step-work entries and sync them across your devices.
  • To generate AI reflections when you choose to use that feature.
  • To operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your recovery content for advertising or marketing.

Third-party services we share data with

To provide core features, the App relies on the following service providers, who process your data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — provides our authentication and cloud database. Your account details and the journal / step-work content you save are stored on Supabase's infrastructure so they can sync across your devices. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI — powers the optional AI reflection feature. When you use that feature, the journal text you submit is sent to OpenAI's API to generate a response, then returned to you. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy and their API data usage policy. If you prefer not to share content with OpenAI, simply don't use the AI feature.

These providers are bound to use the data only to provide their services to us. We do not share your data with any other third parties except where required by law.

Data storage and security

Data transmitted between the App and our service providers is encrypted in transit. Your stored data is protected by the security controls of our infrastructure providers. While no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Data retention

We retain your account and journal data for as long as your account remains active. You can delete individual entries within the App. To delete your entire account and associated data, contact us at aidan.koetaan@gmail.com and we will remove your data from our systems.

Your rights and choices

  • You can access and edit your entries at any time within the App.
  • You can request a copy of your data, or request that we delete it, by emailing us.
  • You can choose not to use the optional AI reflection feature.

Children's privacy

Recovery Companion is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be communicated within the App where appropriate.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, email aidan.koetaan@gmail.com.
