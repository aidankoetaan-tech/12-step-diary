# Google Play listing — Recovery Companion

Copy-paste these into Play Console → Store presence → Main store listing.

## App name (30 chars max)

> Recovery Companion: 12 Steps

## Short description (80 chars max)

> Private 12-step diary: sobriety counter, journal & support circle. No account.

## Full description (4000 chars max)

> **Your recovery, one day at a time — completely private.**
>
> Recovery Companion is a calm, simple companion for anyone working a
> 12-step program. No account, no cloud, no ads — everything you write
> stays on your phone, encrypted.
>
> **🏠 Sobriety counter**
> Set your sobriety date and watch the days add up. See how far you've
> come and how close your next milestone is, with a daily reflection to
> start the day grounded.
>
> **👣 Work the 12 steps**
> All twelve steps, each with its guiding principle. Mark the step you're
> working on, complete it when you're ready, and watch your progress bar
> fill — at your pace. Progress, not perfection.
>
> **📖 Private journal**
> Check in with yourself using gentle prompts ("What am I grateful for
> today?"), track your mood, and write freely. Entries are encrypted on
> your device — nobody can read them but you.
>
> **🤝 Support circle**
> Recovery happens in connection. Keep your sponsor and trusted friends
> one tap away with built-in call and text buttons, plus 24/7 crisis
> lines (SAMHSA, 988) when you need help right now.
>
> **🔒 Privacy first**
> • No account or sign-up
> • No data collection, analytics, or ads
> • Everything stored on-device, encrypted
> • Delete anything, anytime
>
> Whether you're on day 1 or day 1,000: you're not alone, and this
> chapter is called recovery.
>
> *Recovery Companion is a personal diary and support tool, not medical
> advice or treatment. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Alcoholics
> Anonymous World Services or any 12-step fellowship.*
